Explore Our Legacy

Defining Luxury, Setting the Standard.

Since 1886, Mercedes-Benz has been synonymous with visionary technology and incomparable comfort. We don't just follow automotive trends; we invent them. The three-pointed star represents not only the conquest of land, sea, and air but our enduring commitment to mobility.

Our journey began with Karl Benz's Patent Motorwagen, the world's first automobile. That same pioneering spirit lives on in every car we produce today, from the quiet power of our EQ electric vehicles to the raw, exhilarating performance of AMG.

1886

Karl Benz patents the first automobile, laying the foundation for modern transport.

1901

1954

The iconic 300 SL "Gullwing" is introduced, blending performance and art.
