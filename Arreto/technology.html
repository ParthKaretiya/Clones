SHOES THAT
GROW WITH THEM

Kids' feet grow fast. We invented a shoe that keeps up. Discover the Aretto Grooves™ Technology that expands up to 3 whole sizes.

THE INNOVATION

Aretto Grooves™

The secret lies in the sole. Inspired by the blooming of a flower, our patented sole features deep grooves that expand naturally as your child's foot puts pressure on them.

This allows the shoe to widen and lengthen millimetres at a time, providing a perfect fit for up to 18 months.

THE MATERIAL

3D Knit Fabric

Growth isn't just about the sole. Our upper material is crafted from a specialized 3D Stretch Knit. It’s breathable, durable, and most importantly, flexible.

It hugs the foot like a sock, ensuring that even as the foot grows, there are no pinch points or uncomfortable tightness.

THE COMFORT

Squishy Insole

Kids run, jump, and stomp. They need shock absorption. Our memory foam insole adapts to the unique shape of your child's foot arch.

It resets after every wear, meaning the cushioning feels new every single morning.

HOW IT WORKS

01

Millimetric Growth

Unlike standard shoes that jump 10mm per size, Aretto shoes expand proportionally in all directions.

02

3 Sizes in 1

Buy a size 6, and it fits perfectly through sizes 7 and 8. You save money, they stay comfortable.

03

Natural Shape

The shoe mimics the barefoot walking experience, promoting healthy foot development.
